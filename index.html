Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

What does "refurbished" actually mean? +

Refurbished means the printer has been professionally restored to like-new condition. We completely disassemble each unit, replace all worn parts (rollers, fusers, pickup assemblies), deep clean every component, update firmware, and run extensive testing. You get a printer that looks and performs like new, but at 50-70% off the original price.

How long is the warranty? +

All our refurbished printers come with a comprehensive 12-month warranty covering parts and labor. If anything goes wrong, we'll fix it or replace it - no questions asked. We also offer extended warranty options up to 3 years.

How long does delivery take? +

For Johannesburg and surrounding areas: 1-2 business days. For other major cities (Cape Town, Durban, Pretoria): 2-3 business days. Remote areas: 3-5 business days. We offer free delivery on orders over R3,500. You'll receive tracking information once your order ships.

Can I see the printer before buying? +

Absolutely! Visit our showroom at 75 Silwood Avenue, Johannesburg. We're open Monday-Friday 8AM-5PM and Saturday 9AM-2PM. You can see our full inventory, test print quality, and speak with our team. No appointment necessary, but call ahead if you want to see a specific model.

What's included with my printer purchase? +

Every printer includes: the fully refurbished unit, power cable, starter toner cartridge, USB cable (if applicable), setup guide, and 12-month warranty certificate. Network cables and additional toner available for purchase separately.

Do you offer installation and setup? +

Yes! We offer on-site installation and network setup for R500 within Johannesburg. This includes unpacking, connecting to your network, installing drivers on up to 3 computers, and testing. Remote setup assistance is free via phone or WhatsApp.

What if I'm not satisfied with my purchase? +

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied, return the printer in its original condition for a full refund. We just ask that you cover return shipping. Your satisfaction is our priority.

How do I know which printer is right for me? +

Consider your printing needs: Volume (pages per month), type (text only or graphics/photos), features needed (scanning, duplex, color), and budget. Our team is happy to help! WhatsApp us at +27 72 180 4650 or visit our showroom for personalized recommendations.

Do you buy or trade-in old printers? +

Yes! We buy working printers and offer trade-ins toward your purchase. Send us photos and model details via WhatsApp for a quote. We accept HP, Canon, Xerox, Brother, Epson, and Ricoh models. Even non-working printers - we'll recycle them responsibly.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept: EFT/Bank Transfer, Cash (in-store only), Credit/Debit Cards via PayFast, SnapScan, and Zapper. For corporate clients, we offer 30-day payment terms subject to credit approval.
